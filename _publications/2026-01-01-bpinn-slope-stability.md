A slope-stability classifier that cannot contradict the mechanics it is meant to represent. Monotonicity with respect to six governing parameters is enforced in the architecture rather than through a training penalty, so every posterior draw respects it, including outside the training range. The network is pretrained on 2,000 of 3,000 automated PLAXIS 2D strength-reduction runs and fine-tuned on a screened database of 275 published circular-failure cases, with 55 held back as a locked test set.

Joint first-author work with R. A. Nishat. **Status:** manuscript in preparation.
